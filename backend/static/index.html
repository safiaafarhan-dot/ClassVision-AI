A camera, a pipeline and the attendance decision, running on the machine in the room. What you are looking at is the shape of a classroom: the people in it, what the system can see, and what it has decided.

                                            What it will not do

                                              Academic terms

                                              Years and semesters, and moving students into them. A student is permanent; their class, section and roll number are facts about a period. Promoting creates a new period and never edits the one before it, which is what keeps last term’s attendance describing the class a student was actually in when it was taken. Creating a term touches nobody and promotes nobody.

                                              Terms

                                              Exactly one term is current. Activating a term closes the previous one rather than deleting it — its enrolments and its attendance stay exactly where they were.

                                              No academic term has been created

                                              Sessions recorded with no term still work and are reported as unassigned. A term is what lets a student’s class and roll number change at the end of a year without rewriting the register behind them.
